meteorite classifier

Paste or upload a visible–near-infrared reflectance spectrum and this ROCKET model will place it in one of 16 meteorite groups. It's the model from my meteorite spectra project, trained on 1,310 RELAB lab spectra. Everything runs in your browser; your spectrum is never uploaded anywhere.

Spectrum

Two columns, wavelength and reflectance, separated by spaces, tabs, or commas. Wavelengths can be in µm or nm and must cover 0.535–2.325 µm. Extra columns and lines starting with # are ignored.

    How it works

    The spectrum is interpolated onto a 0.005 µm grid from 0.535 to 2.325 µm and divided by its reflectance at 0.80 µm. ROCKET applies 10,000 random convolutional kernels and keeps two summary numbers from each, then a Ridge classifier scores the resulting 20,000 features. The training set was augmented to 200 spectra per group with PCA jitter.

    The model learned from RELAB lab spectra of meteorite samples, and the examples above are from that training set. Spectra measured with a different instrument, grain size, or viewing geometry may be classified less reliably.
